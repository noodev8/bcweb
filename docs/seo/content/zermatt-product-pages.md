## 1. The shared text (same on all 12 pages)

### The Birkenstock Zermatt

The Zermatt is Birkenstock's wool felt slipper, and one of our best sellers here at Brookfield Comfort. It's on the same cork-latex
footbed as the sandals and Birkenstock lovers feel comfortable straight away. It's a house shoe, and we have two versions:

- **Zermatt (Standard Footbed)** — wool felt upper with a suede-lined footbed,
  the same feel underfoot as a Birkenstock sandal.
- **Zermatt Shearling** — the same wool felt slipper with a shearling-lined
  footbed. The warmer, cosier one, and the one most people keep for indoors.

### What size Zermatt should I get?

**Our customers normally go with their standard Birkenstock size.** If
you're unsure or between sizes, going up one size is normal.

We like ours with socks; plenty of customers prefer them barefoot and no issue keeping warm. If you're a
socks person and between sizes, that's when going up makes the most sense.

New to Birkenstock? Our [Birkenstock size guide](/pages/birkenstock-size-guide)
converts UK to EU sizes. Birkenstock don't make a UK 6 —
[here's why, and which size to pick](/blogs/info/why-doesnt-birkenstock-make-a-uk-size-6-brookfield-comfort).

### Narrow or regular fit?

Birkenstock's regular width is the most popular and often used by men, especially in the larger sizes. The narrow fit is snug, for a slim foot. Plenty of women wear regular too. If you don't know which you are, start with regular.

(Changed 2026-11-07, same day as going live — the "Most women between UK 3 and UK 7 want narrow" line was dropped: our sales don't back it. Zermatt EU 35–40, 2 years: narrow 144, regular 156.)

### The footbed

The footbed lifts out of the slipper — it's removable and can be replaced. Like a sandal, the iconic cork moulds to the shape of your foot as you wear it in.

### Colours

Light grey is the most popular with women while darker is sold more to men, although the width and colours are truly mixed across everyone. New Zermatt colours are introduced on occasion but the standard colours have remained strong.

### Where your pair comes from

We have our Zermatts delivered directly from Birkenstock in Germany. All stored and dispatched by our team at our Mid Wales warehouse. Dispatched and tracked across the country with Royal Mail, including 24hr delivery options. We are proud to be Birkenstock retailers and work closely with the brand.
