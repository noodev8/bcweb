# Graphic prompt — cut and paste

One prompt to paste into an image generator. It makes a **background plate** —
an empty scene with space reserved in the middle. You drop the real product
photo into that space afterwards.

**It does not draw the shoe, and it must not.** An image generator asked for a
Birkenstock produces a plausible-looking sandal with wrong straps, wrong buckles
and a fake logo. Posting that as a real brand's product misleads the customer and
is a trademark problem. The shoe is always your photograph.

---

## Paste this

```
Create a square 1:1 social media background plate for a footwear retailer.

An empty product-photography scene: a warm neutral surface — pale sand-toned
matte stone with a very subtle texture — filling the lower two thirds of the
frame, and a plain, softly out-of-focus off-white wall behind it. Soft natural
daylight falling from the upper left, with gentle diffused shadows.

Leave the centre of the frame completely empty and unobstructed. A product will
be placed there later, so nothing may occupy or overlap that space.

Muted, natural, earthy palette. Calm and understated, not glossy, not
high-contrast, not commercial-looking. Photographic, shallow depth of field.

No text, no lettering, no watermarks, no logos, no products, no shoes, no people,
no hands, no plants, no props.
```

---

## Variations

Swap one line to get a set that looks like a family without being identical.
Change the **surface**, keep everything else:

- `pale sand-toned matte stone` (default)
- `pale oak floorboards, light and softly grained`
- `natural undyed linen, lightly creased`
- `weathered pale timber, softly bleached`
- `sun-warmed terracotta tile, matte`

And optionally the **light**:

- `soft natural daylight falling from the upper left` (default)
- `low golden afternoon light from the right, long soft shadows`

Generate 5 or 6 and keep the ones that match each other. Consistency across the
set is what makes a feed look deliberate rather than random.

---

## If it goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| It put a shoe in anyway | Add: `absolutely no footwear of any kind in the image` |
| It added text or a logo | Add: `no text of any kind, no signage, no branding` |
| Too busy / styled | Add: `extremely minimal, almost empty, plain` |
| Centre is cluttered | Add: `the middle 60% of the image must be plain empty surface` |
| Too shiny / advert-looking | Add: `natural, documentary, not advertising photography` |

---

## Finishing the post

1. Generate the plate.
2. Place your real product photo in the centre — cut out, or a clean photo shot
   against a similar background.
3. Add the delivery line as text in a lower corner, small and understated:
   **Order by 2pm Mon–Fri — next working day delivery**
4. Keep that text in the same place, same size, same font on every post. It
   becomes recognisable.

Square 1:1 works on both Facebook and Instagram. 1080×1080 is enough.
