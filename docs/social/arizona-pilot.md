# Birkenstock Arizona — pilot post set

Eight posts covering the rotation. At 2–3 a week that is ~3 weeks of content.

**Nothing here is scheduled or approved.** Draft copy for review.

## The rule: no perishable facts

**Posts contain only what is true all year.** No counts, no prices, no named
colours, no "X in stock now".

A count is not just a maintenance job — it goes stale and becomes quietly
dishonest. Checked Jul 2026: 60 Arizona colour/width combinations in stock, but a
long tail of them down to one or two sizes. "60 colours" would be technically
true and practically misleading, and it would be a different number next month.

**Durable facts — safe to state, no query needed:**

- We stock Arizona in depth, in both regular and narrow fit
- Sizes UK 2.5–13
- Materials: Birko-Flor, EVA, Birkibuc, patent, natural leather, vegan
- Order by 2pm Mon–Fri, next working day

Anything beyond that list needs checking, which means it does not belong in a
reusable post.

## Honest read on this set

Posts 2, 4, 6 and 7 are the strong ones — they say something useful and are the
kind of thing someone saves or sends on. Those are also the ones that survive
being reused for other collections with the specifics swapped.

Posts 1 and 5 are the weakest. They are "we have stock" dressed up, and there is
a limit to how many times that can be said before it is obviously the same post.
That is the real constraint on this whole idea: **the useful-information posts
scale, the we-have-stock posts do not.** If the rotation drifts towards the
latter, the feed will go stale regardless of how good the images are.

Implication for the wider rollout: the durable template is not "brand + delivery
+ link", it is **one genuinely useful thing about the product, then delivery,
then link.** That needs a small amount of real knowledge per collection — which
you have and I do not.
