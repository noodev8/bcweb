How our Amazon prices are set

One page to read before you price Amazon. Everything is on Amazon Pricing (Daily → Amazon to price). BCWEB does the checking and works out the price. You decide: Set or Leave.

1

Amazon's shop window

Lots of sellers sell the same shoe on Amazon. When a customer opens it, Amazon puts one seller in the shop window: the price and the "Add to basket" button. That's called the Buy Box.

Most customers buy from whoever is in the window. So our aim is simple: be in the window, at the best price we can get.

2

Every hour, BCWEB checks Amazon

For every size we have in stock at Amazon, it looks at:

  • who is in the window, and at what price
  • the other sellers and their prices
  • whether the size sold in the last 30 days
  • how much we keep a pair at each price
  • the day the size first landed at Amazon

Only sizes in stock at Amazon get a card. Sizes still on their way, or sold out, don't: they go by their review date (step 6). When a size sells out, Amazon Order handles the restock.

3

Every size gets one answer

First, BCWEB asks: is this size new? A size is new from the day it lands at Amazon until its first sale, or for 30 days if it doesn’t sell. New sizes have their own tab with one job: get the first sale. If someone else is in the window, the card is a penny under them, never below break-even. After the first sale it’s like any other size.

RaiseWe're in the window and the other sellers are well above us. We can go up a little and stay in it.
CutSomeone else is in the window. We can go under them and still keep at least £3 a pair. Only sizes that sold in the last 30 days.
LeaveNothing to gain. We're already in the best place, or the other seller is too cheap to follow.
Not sellingIt sold nothing on Amazon in 30 days, and another seller isn't the reason. It goes by its review date instead (step 6).

Only New, Raise and Cut make cards. New sizes have their own tab, so they're never mixed with the rest. BCWEB works out the exact price for you. A selling size has no review date to wait for: it shows up the moment it has a card.

4

Pick a mood, then price

The mood changes how hard we push. You pick it at the top of Amazon Pricing, before you open the cards. It starts on Chilled every time. Pick the one that fits the day: Fierce to shift stock, Climb to reach for more margin.

The New, Raise and Cut tiles show how many cards there are in that mood. Price → opens the cards with the mood already chosen; it shows at the top. To change it, press Change: it takes you back to Amazon Pricing. (Daily's number always counts Chilled.)

Set

  • The price goes to Amazon straight away.
  • The card goes until BCWEB has checked Amazon again (within the hour).
  • Its review date moves 4 weeks out. That only matters if the size stops selling.

Leave

  • Nothing changes on Amazon.
  • The card comes back if the suggested price changes, or after 7 days.
  • Its review date moves 4 weeks out, as with Set.

Other keys: E type your own price · ← back a card · U undo.

6

Then Not selling · due

The fourth tile on Amazon Pricing. These are the sizes the Buy Box can't help with: sold nothing in 30 days, still on their way to Amazon, or out of stock. They work the way you're used to: a list with a review date. A size shows when its date comes round. Price it, or set a new review date, from the list or its price screen.

The next check shows what your move did. A cut that put us back in the window counts as won back. A raise that kept us in the window counts as raise kept. You'll see both on Team week.

Always, in every mood

  • Never below £3 profit a pair. New sizes, until their first sale: never below break-even.
  • Never above the RRP.
  • One step at a time. After a raise, the size waits until a pair sells before it can go up again.

Your routine

  1. Open Amazon Pricing (Daily → Amazon to price) and pick today's mood. It starts on Chilled.
  2. Price the cards: New, then Raise, then Cut. Set or Leave every card.
  3. Then Not selling · due. Price it or set a review date.

When Daily's Amazon number is 0, you're done. Need to change a size that isn't on a card? Find it and price it by hand, as you do now.
